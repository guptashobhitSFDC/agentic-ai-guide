The shift from AI that answers to AI that acts.

Based on Stanford's free CS329A lecture series (Akanksha & Azalia Mirhoseini) — turned into a short guided course you can finish in about 40 minutes, no video required. Pick the lane that fits your role and I'll tailor it to your work.

The one idea to hold onto If you remember nothing else: agents get powerful through a loop of act → check → improve. The whole course — and most of the hard problems — come back to verification and feedback: where AI can reliably check its own work, it can run on its own; where it can't, a human stays accountable. Everything else builds on that.
